Time Turner — Personal Planner

A learner's Time Turner week

The same two-week planner every Time Turner student sees, populated with a real 10-day rotating timetable instead of typed-in class times — school blocks come straight from the timetable below; everything else (homework, sport, chores, family time, rest) is what a student would add themselves.

Source: Curro Durbanville High School, Grade 8A1, Term 3 2026 (uploaded timetable, generated 20/7/2026). Period codes decoded using the school's own subject names already on record in Time Turner (e.g. BUS01 → Business Studies, LSC01 → Life Sciences; AFR03 → Afrikaans Huistaal, ENG01 → English First Additional Language). This cycle falls within a test series. The six periods never change — the same Period 1–6 timetable repeats every day regardless. What changes on Tuesdays and Thursdays (Days 2, 4, 7, 9) is the break pattern and an added test period: Periods 1–3, break, Periods 4–6, break, then a test period appended at the end of the day (illustrative length), rather than Monday/Wednesday/Friday's breaks after Period 2 and Period 4. The test period's subject is Amahle's actual due assessment that day from the calendar (Dramatic Arts, Visual Arts, Coding and Robotics, Life Sciences) — it doesn't replace any of the six regular periods. Mapped onto 24 Aug – 4 Sept 2026 so due dates line up with the "One Learner, Five Grades" assessment mockup.
