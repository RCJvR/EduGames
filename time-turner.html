📆 Upload your timetable
Paste your school's 10-day, 6-period timetable once instead of adding each class by hand — this works the same whether it's your class timetable or your own teaching timetable. One row per timetable day (1–10): the day number, then each period's subject, separated by commas — leave a period blank for a free period or break. Uses the period times set up above, so set those first.

🪄 Add a block
Use Timetable days for the things that rotate with your school's 10-day cycle — usually just class/school hours. Use Every week for everything else: practices, tests, meals, chores, sleep — they happen on the same real day each week, not by cycle day.

📤 Export to your calendar
Weekly items (practices, meals, sleep) export as recurring events with no setup. Timetable-day blocks need the real date of Day 1 to know which calendar dates they fall on. Anything you ticked "🔔 Remind me the evening before" on comes through as a real alarm — once imported, your phone's own calendar app handles the notification, on iPhone or Android, with nothing else to set up.
